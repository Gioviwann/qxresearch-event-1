# Contexto: World Experience — guide bike on English price list

Ops note for the English rate card / product sheet for **World Experience** (partner listed under B2B contacts / ciclotour). Assimilated from Gio’s wording (Nov 2026).

## Copy to paste (EN)

**Guide e-bike**  
One guide bike is free per group for the first two groups. From the third group onwards, the guide pays for the e-bike used on that service. The guide’s helmet remains included.

### Shorter rate-card line

Guide e-bike: complimentary for the first two groups (one free guide bike per group). From the third group, the guide e-bike is charged. Guide helmet included.

### Bullet form

- First two groups: **1 guide e-bike free** per group  
- From the **3rd group**: guide e-bike is **payable** (same service)  
- Guide **helmet**: always **included**
