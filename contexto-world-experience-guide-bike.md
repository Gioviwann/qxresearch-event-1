## Partner reality (WE)

World Experience mainly **rents bikes / books activities** with A-Bike. Past financial issues → **every activity prepaid**. Their clients often come from **cruises**; no-shows happen. They sell via **Viator / DMCs**, which often do **not pay them** if the activity does not run — so they tend **not to book** when rain is forecast. A-Bike T&Cs must stay fair to WE but protect prepaid inventory and no-shows.

**Verdict on Gio’s shortened list:** directionally **yes** (prepaid deadlines, no refund language on reductions, no-show = charged). Tighten three gaps: (1) increases only / reductions not refunded after payment; (2) weather — confirmed bookings run rain or shine unless **A-Bike** cancels for AEMET red; if **WE** cancels for rain/OTA, cancellation rules apply; (3) keep shop addresses.

### Bookings & confirmation

- Bookings by email to **barcelona@a-bike.es**. We confirm within **24 hours** on working days.
- Last-minute requests are welcome for daily and private tours, subject to availability and the payment deadlines below.
- **Groups and MICE** must be booked **at least one month** in advance, with **full payment** of the activity at the same time.
- Departures start and finish at the same shop — **Carrer dels Tallers 45** or **Carrer de Montserrat 8** — as confirmed with the booking.

### Payment

- Invoices are issued on confirmation and payable by **bank transfer**.
- **Daily tours:** paid in full **at least 24 hours** before the start of the service.
- **Private tours:** paid in full **at least 2 days** before the start of the service.
- **Groups and MICE:** booked and paid in full **at least one month** in advance, at the prices and conditions on this card.

### Group size & changes

- Participant numbers may be adjusted **free of charge** up to **48 hours** before the tour.
- **Increases** are invoiced at the rates on this card and must be paid before the service.
- **Reductions** after payment are **not refunded** (credit may be agreed only in writing).
- Delivery and collection are not included unless quoted separately.

### Cancellation & no-shows

- Free cancellation up to **48 hours** before the start.
- Within 48 hours, and for **no-shows**, the booking is charged in full (no refund of amounts already paid).
- Museums, restaurants and attractions keep their own terms. Our cut-off is the provider’s deadline **plus two business days**.

### Weather

- Once booked and paid, services run **rain or shine**.
- **Ponchos** can be provided at the **same price as the helmet** on this card.
- **We** cancel only under an official **AEMET red** weather warning, with a **full refund**.
- If **you** cancel because of weather, Viator/DMC rules, or soft rain forecasts, the cancellation terms above apply.

### Bike transport

- Bike transport is quoted **per job** or **per day**. It is run by a third party. Previous fixed van prices no longer apply.

### Seasons & shop hours

- **Low season:** 1 October – 31 March (except weekends and bank holidays). Shop hours **09:00–18:00**.
- **High season:** 1 April – **30 September**, plus weekends and bank holidays. Shop hours **09:00–20:00**.
- Rates apply during shop opening hours. Work outside these hours is charged as an extra, on quote.

### VAT & commercial terms

- Prices include **21% IVA** (Spanish VAT).
- **No commission:** group and rental rates on this card are already **discounted net** rates.
- Add-ons (restaurants, venues, entries, transport, etc.) are **pass-through** costs and carry no commission.
