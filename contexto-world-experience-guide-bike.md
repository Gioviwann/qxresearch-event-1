## Guide e-bike (copy)

**Guide e-bike**  
One guide bike is free per group for the first two groups. From the third group onwards, the guide pays for the e-bike used on that service. The guide’s helmet remains included.

## Guides / languages (copy)

Please confirm guide requirements at least **24 hours** in advance. We can guarantee **English**-speaking guides. For **French**, **Italian**, **Portuguese**, **German** or **Dutch**, please notify us at least **1 week** in advance.

## Terms & conditions (reorganised EN — for WE)

Friendly tone for the partner; commercial protections kept. Fixed: high season ends **30 September** (not 31st).

### Bookings & confirmation

- Bookings by email to **barcelona@a-bike.es**. We confirm within **24 hours** on working days. Last-minute requests are welcome, subject to availability.
- Departures start and finish at the same shop — **Carrer dels Tallers 45** or **Carrer de Montserrat 8** — as confirmed with the booking.

### Group size & changes

- Participant numbers may be adjusted **free of charge** up to **48 hours** before the tour. Any difference is refunded or invoiced at the rates on this card.
- Groups already confirmed at **2026 rates** keep their confirmed price. Delivery and collection are not included unless quoted separately.

### Cancellation

- Free cancellation up to **48 hours** before the start.
- Within 48 hours, and for no-shows, the booking is charged in full.
- Museums, restaurants and attractions keep their own terms. Our cut-off is the provider’s deadline **plus two business days**.

### Weather

- Tours run **rain or shine**; ponchos are provided.
- We cancel only under an official **AEMET red** weather warning, with a **full refund**.

### Payment

- **All services** must be paid in full **at least 24 hours before** the start of the service.
- Invoices are issued on confirmation and payable by **bank transfer**.
- **Daily** and **private** tours: paid in advance, no later than 24 hours before departure.
- **MICE**: prices and conditions on this card; payable **one month** in advance (and in any case before the 24-hour cut-off above).

### Bike transport

- Bike transport is quoted **per job** or **per day**. It is run by a third party. Previous fixed van prices no longer apply.

### Seasons & shop hours

- **Low season:** 1 October – 31 March (except weekends and bank holidays). Shop hours **09:00–18:00**.
- **High season:** 1 April – **30 September**, plus weekends and bank holidays. Shop hours **09:00–20:00**.
- Rates apply during shop opening hours. Work outside these hours is charged as an extra, on quote.

### VAT & commercial terms

- Prices include **21% IVA** (Spanish VAT).
- **No commission:** group and rental rates on this card are already **discounted net** rates. Add-ons (restaurants, venues, entries, transport, etc.) are **pass-through** costs and carry no commission.
